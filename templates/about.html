About Aurelia Grand

Hospitality with quiet confidence.

Our Story

Founded in 2009 by a family of Tashkent hoteliers, Aurelia Grand began as a 40-room townhouse. Today it holds 180 rooms and suites, but we kept the original habit: greet every guest personally and never rush a meal.

Our philosophy is simple. Comfort comes first, then craft, then detail. We prefer fewer, better things.

Mission

To give every guest a calm, well-run stay that feels personal rather than scripted.

Vision

To be the city's most trusted address for travellers who value privacy and service.

Why Choose Us

Our hospitality philosophy
